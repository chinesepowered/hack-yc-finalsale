### Try-on images

The images in `public/demo/results/` were generated by the YouCam API (AI Clothes `cloth-v4`, AI Shoes
`shoes-transfer`) from the photos above on Nov 1, 2026. Each is an adaptation of its source photos and keeps their
attribution. The images made from a CC BY-SA source are shared under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/):

- `maya__gingham-dress.jpg`, `maya__gingham-dress__white-sneakers.jpg` (dress by Rhododendrites)
- `maya__denim-jacket__white-sneakers.jpg`, `maya__gingham-dress__white-sneakers.jpg` (sneakers by JustinLiang522)

The demo video `demo/video.mp4` and thumbnail `demo/thumb.jpg` show these images and carry the same attributions.
